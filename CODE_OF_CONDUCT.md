# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and every other space of this project.

- Assume good faith, and criticise code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing of other people's private information.
- Keep to the topic; a short, clear bug report or patch is worth more than a long argument.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who repeatedly break these rules.

Report a problem through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability" opens a private channel) or by contacting the maintainer, [@leongreiner](https://github.com/leongreiner), on GitHub.
